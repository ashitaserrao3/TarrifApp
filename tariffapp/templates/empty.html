Get started

No flight schedule loaded yet. To get going:

  1. Upload the flight-schedule workbook (the one with the Raw Data sheet). Its station list, add-on rates and network surcharges are imported too.
  2. Upload your Air India and IndiGo rate-list files (as many cycles as you have – history is kept).
  3. Come back here to look up any route.

Already have the old CargoRates database? Import all its history in one go with flask --app tariffapp import-legacy path\to\CargoRates.accdb (see README).
